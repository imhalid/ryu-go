# Separator

A groove dividing content: a dark line and a light one beside it, cut into
the surface rather than drawn on it. With a `Label` it parts around a word;
`Vertical` stands it up, in a row or a toolbar.

<!-- preview separator 240 -->

```go
ryugo.Separator(c, ryugo.SeparatorOptions{Label: "or"})
ryugo.Separator(c, ryugo.SeparatorOptions{Vertical: true})
```
